# 26.39-4613

- Space availability customization: you can now control when each space is available. This also means Morning & Night Space schedules can now be customized.
- Away, Morning and Night Spaces can now be turned off.
- School, Active and Focus Spaces are now disabled by default.
- Private Space support: this includes multiple ways of accessing and controlling Private Space through Spaces, gestures, folders and more.
- Per-Space gestures: you can now customize gesture actions for each individual Space.
- New Quick Settings tile: tap to cycle to the next available space, long press to open Spaces settings.
- New charging indicator on Glance with dynamic animations based on the charging state.
- Custom Default Apps: override which apps open when tapping on the time and date texts on Glance.
- Hide status bar: globally or per space.
- Dock & Glance are now more compact and take less screen space.
- Theme Sync: first-party apps can now follow Yasan Launcher's theme automatically.
- Major improvements to Yasan Launcher Intelligence.
- New Recents gesture action.
- Hidden apps revealed in search now carry a badge.
- Folder app management screen now shows the selected apps in a horizontal list instead of vertical.
- App badges now use a more standard shape and size.
- Settings screen now shows in recent apps.
- Feedback screen is brought back but now uses email.
- Many UI/UX improvements including major changes to Space settings to make them easier to read at a glance and navigate.
- Improved reliability for location-based Space automations.
- Improved reliability for the home grid & widgets.
- Major performance improvements & bug fixes.
